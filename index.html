About this map

Roads & Railways of Armenia is a free interactive map of the country's road and rail network, built from OpenStreetMap. Explore motorways, Interstate (M), National (H) and Local (T) roads, tracks and paths by surface, speed limit, lighting and track grade, and railways by gauge, electrification and usage.

Updated monthly. Data © OpenStreetMap contributors, ODbL.

What do the M, H and T road codes mean?

In Armenia, M roads are interstate (international) highways, H roads are national (republican) roads and T roads are local roads. Each road's ref tag is shown in its popup.

Where does the data come from?

Everything comes from OpenStreetMap, the free community-built map. The page loads a monthly snapshot, or queries OpenStreetMap live if none is available. Spot a mistake? Use the edit link in any popup.

Can I download the data?

Yes. Export what you see as GeoJSON or CSV, or use the open files in the data folder. The data is under the ODbL licence: credit OpenStreetMap contributors.
